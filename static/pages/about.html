I am Amit Jangra, currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Delhi Technological University (DTU). I previously served as a Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering at Government Polytechnic, Hisar since April 2023. I have taught a range of core subjects including Workshop Technology, Mechanical Engineering Drawing, CNC Machines & Automation, and related laboratory courses. My academic background is rooted in Thermal Engineering, and my research interests lie at the intersection of computational methods and experimental analysis, with a particular focus on microfluidics. I am especially interested in leveraging machine learning techniques to enhance modeling, simulation, and performance optimization in thermal-fluid systems and micro-scale flows.
